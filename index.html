Common Scams in India

Sabse zyada hone wale cyber frauds — Hindi, Hinglish aur English mein samjhein. Apno ko share karein.

हिंदीHinglish
💸 OTP Fraud / OTP ठगी
🔴 "Sir, aapka account band hoga — OTP batao ABHI!"
Bank ya UIDAI ke naam se call. "KYC expire" bol ke daraaate hain. OTP dete hi account saaf.
⚠ RED FLAGSUrgency create karna. Bank kabhi OTP phone par nahin maangta.
✓ BACHAVOTP kisi ko na batao — bank ko bhi nahin. Call aaye to rakho aur official number se callback karo.
हिंदीHinglish
📲 Fake Loan App Fraud
🔴 "तुरंत लोन — कोई document नहीं, कोई CIBIL नहीं!"
Fake app install karaate hain. Contacts, photos access lete hain. Chhoti loan deke harassment — family ko morphed photos ki dhamki.
⚠ RED FLAGSPlay Store se bahar APK, zyada permissions, 1 din mein loan — sab fraud ke signs.
✓ BACHAVSirf RBI registered lenders. App permissions check karo. Harassment ho to 1930 call karo turant.
हिंदीHinglish
📈 Telegram Investment Scam
🔴 "हमारे group join करो — रोज़ ₹5000 Guaranteed!"
Stock tips group mein add karte hain. Pehle chhota profit dikhate hain (fake dashboard). Phir bada invest karaate hain — gaayab.
⚠ RED FLAGSGuaranteed returns, SEBI registered nahin, Telegram/WhatsApp group, withdrawal blocked.
✓ BACHAVSEBI.gov.in par verify karo. Guaranteed returns = guaranteed fraud. Sirf registered broker use karo.
हिंदीHinglish
📱 UPI / QR Code Scam
🔴 "QR scan karo — ₹1000 aapke account mein aayenge!"
QR scan karne se money receive nahin hoti — send hoti hai. Ya payment request bhejte hain jo log confuse hokar approve kar dete hain.
⚠ RED FLAGSPaisa RECEIVE karne ke liye QR scan ya PIN ki zaroorat NAHIN hoti. Kabhi nahin.
✓ BACHAVQR sirf pay karne ke liye. PIN sirf deney ke liye. Receive karne mein kuch nahin karna hota.
हिंदीHinglish
🪪 KYC Update Fraud
🔴 "KYC expire — अभी update करें नहीं तो account बंद!"
SMS ya email aata hai ki bank/telecom KYC expire hua. Link pe click karaate hain. Aadhaar, PAN, OTP sab le lete hain.
⚠ RED FLAGSSMS mein link bhej kar bank KYC update nahin karaata. Official app ya branch hamesha.
✓ BACHAVLinks par click na karo. Bank number Google par mat dhoondo — card ke peeche se lo ya official site se.
हिंदीHinglish
💚 WhatsApp Hacking
🔴 "Bhai galti se code chala gaya — OTP forward karo!"
Dost ke number se message. WhatsApp verification OTP maangta hai. Dete hi aapka WhatsApp hack. Phir aapke contacts se paise maangta hai.
⚠ RED FLAGSWhatsApp ka 6-digit code KISI ko nahin dena — chahe close dost bhi ho.
✓ BACHAVTwo-Step Verification on karo. OTP share nahin karo. Hack ho to cybercrime.gov.in par report karo.
हिंदीHinglish
🖥 Fake APK / Screen Share Scam
🔴 "Ye app install karo — problem 5 minutes mein solve!"
Fake customer care ka Google number milta hai. AnyDesk/TeamViewer install karaate hain. Screen share se seedha banking app kholke transaction kar lete hain.
⚠ RED FLAGSKoi bhi bank remote access app install karaane ko nahin kehta. Kabhi nahin.
✓ BACHAVScreen access kisi ko mat do. Bank number sirf card ke peeche ya official website se lo.
हिंदीHinglish
📡 SIM Swap Fraud
🔴 अचानक mobile network चला जाता है — कुछ घंटों में account खाली!
Thug pehle personal info collect karta hai. Phir telecom store mein fake ID se duplicate SIM leta hai. Sab OTPs uske phone par aate hain.
⚠ RED FLAGSNetwork achanak band ho jaaye — turant bank call karo aur account freeze karo.
✓ BACHAVTelecom mein SIM change ke liye biometric ensure karo. Bank mein SIM change alert activate karo.
हिंदीHinglish
📦 Digital Arrest / Parcel Scam
🔴 "Sir, parcel mein drugs — CBI arrest karega! Abhi ₹50,000 bhejo!"
FedEx/Customs/Police ke naam se call. Video call par uniform mein bithate hain. Ghanton tak "Digital Arrest" mein rakh ke lakhs thag lete hain.
⚠ RED FLAGS"Digital Arrest" exist hi nahin karta. Police phone par arrest nahin karti. Paise nahin maangti.
✓ BACHAVCall turant kaato. 1930 call karo. PM Modi ne bhi is fraud ko publicly warn kiya hai.
हिंदीHinglish
🤖 AI Voice Cloning Scam
🔴 "Beta, accident ho gaya — ₹50,000 ABHI bhejo!"
AI se family member ki voice clone karte hain — sirf 3 second se. Phone karte hain "emergency" bol ke. Paisa transfer karo, tab pata chalta hai.
⚠ RED FLAGSFamiliar voice but unknown number. "Transfer karo, explain later." Third party account.
✓ BACHAVHamesha us insaan ke known number par callback karo pehle. AI voice clone kar sakta hai, identity verify nahin.
हिंदीHinglish
💼 Fake Job / Task Fraud
🔴 "WFH job — Google reviews rate karo, ₹500 per task!"
Pehle tasks actually pay karte hain. Phir "premium tasks unlock" ke liye deposit maango. ₹10K bante ₹1L — phir manager gaayab.
⚠ RED FLAGSTelegram/WhatsApp job offer, koi interview nahin, pehle deposit karo — 100% fraud.
✓ BACHAVLegitimate jobs KABHI pehle deposit nahin maangti. MCA portal par company verify karo.

Recent Real-World Cyber Frauds

Ye real fraud patterns hain jो India mein hue. Names changed for privacy. Indicators seekhein — trap mein mat aana.

📞
Voice Scam · Lucknow 2024
Fake Bank Customer Care — ₹2.8 Lakh Lost
A 58-year-old teacher Googled "SBI customer care number." The top result was fake. The fraudster impersonated an SBI executive, asked to "verify account" and walked her through downloading AnyDesk. Within 20 minutes ₹2.8 lakh was gone from her savings account.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
Google search results for bank numbers are frequently manipulated
Legitimate banks NEVER ask to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer
Creating urgency — "your account blocks in 2 hours"
✓ Bank number only from back of debit card or official bank website — NEVER Google
📹
Digital Arrest Scam · Pan India 2024
Fake CBI Video Call — ₹12 Lakh Lost
A retired officer from Pune received a video call from men in police uniforms (fake background). Accused of money laundering. Kept on video call for 36 hours straight, threatening arrest. He transferred ₹12 lakh before his daughter intervened. PM Modi publicly warned about this scam.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
"Digital Arrest" is NOT a legal concept — does not exist in Indian law
Real police never demand money via UPI to avoid arrest
Keeping victim isolated on video call for hours — psychological pressure
✓ Hang up immediately. Call 1930. Real courts send physical notices — not video calls.
🤖
AI Voice Cloning · Hyderabad 2024
Cloned Son's Voice — ₹50,000 Transferred
A businessman received a call sounding exactly like his son — crying, saying he had an accident and needed ₹50,000 immediately in a "friend's" account. The AI-cloned voice was created from 3 seconds of his son's Instagram reel. He transferred before calling his son directly.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
Familiar voice but calling from unknown number
Extreme urgency — "Transfer now, explain later"
Asking to send money to third party account (not son's account)
✓ Always call back on the person's known number before any transfer. AI clones voice, not identity.
💰
Pig Butchering Scam · Bengaluru 2024
WhatsApp Trading Group — ₹40 Lakh Lost
A software engineer was added to a WhatsApp group called "Rakesh Jhunjhunwala Trading Tips." Fake insider tips. Invested ₹5,000 first, saw ₹12,000 "profit" in dashboard. Over 4 months, invested ₹40 lakh. The app vanished overnight.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
Added to WhatsApp/Telegram group by unknown person
Fake celebrity endorsement (Rakesh Jhunjhunwala is deceased)
Profits shown on custom app — not SEBI-registered platform
✓ Only invest via SEBI-registered brokers. Verify at sebi.gov.in. Guaranteed returns = guaranteed scam.
💌
Sextortion · Delhi 2024
Fake Matrimonial Profile — Morphed Photo Blackmail
A woman matched with a "NRI doctor" on a matrimonial app. After weeks of video calls (pre-recorded), he sent a morphed intimate photo claiming it was her and demanded ₹1 lakh. She had never shared any such photo — it was made from her public social media pictures.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
Profile too perfect — NRI, doctor, high salary, widowed
Never video calls in real-time — uses pre-recorded videos
Morphed photos made from public social media profile pictures
✓ Don't pay. File under S.66E IT Act at cybercrime.gov.in. NCW helpline: 7827170170.
📦
Phishing · Pan India 2024
Fake KYC SMS Link — Bank Account Drained
A retired employee received an SMS: "Your SBI KYC expires today. Click here: sbi-kyc-update.com." She clicked, entered Aadhaar and OTP. Within minutes, ₹1.5 lakh was transferred from her account in 3 transactions. The domain was registered that same week.
⚠ SCAM INDICATORS
Unofficial domain name — not sbi.co.in but sbi-kyc-update.com
Banks never send KYC update links via SMS
Urgency: "expires today"
✓ Check URL carefully. Bank KYC only via official app or bank branch visit.

// LEGAL FRAMEWORK

India's Cyber Laws

IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023 and IT Rules 2021 explained simply — know your rights and the penalties for offenders.

IT ACT 2000

Information Technology Act

enacted 2000 · amended 2008

India's primary cybercrime legislation covering hacking, data theft, identity theft, obscenity and cyber terrorism. The 2008 amendment added critical sections.

// Quick Facts
Sections90+
Nodal BodyCERT-In
Max Fine₹5 Crore
Max JailLife
§43
Unauthorised Computer Access
Civil liability — up to ₹1 crore
›

Civil liability for unauthorised access, data theft, virus introduction and system disruption.

  • Accessing computer resource without permission
  • Downloading or extracting data without authorisation
  • Introducing computer virus or contaminant
  • Disrupting a computer network
⚠ Civil PenaltyDamages up to ₹1,00,00,000 to affected person.
§43A
Sensitive Personal Data — Corporate Liability
Negligence in data protection by companies
›

Corporate bodies liable for negligence in protecting sensitive personal data of individuals. ISO 27001 considered as reasonable security practice.

  • Applies to all body corporates handling personal data
  • Liability arises from negligence — intent not required
  • No upper limit on compensation
§66
Hacking / Computer Related Offences
Criminal counterpart of Section 43
›

Criminalises acts under Section 43 when done dishonestly or fraudulently — the key criminal hacking provision.

⚠ Criminal PenaltyImprisonment up to 3 years, or fine up to ₹5,00,000, or both.
§66B–F
Identity Theft, Cheating, Privacy, Terrorism
Specific cybercrime offences added in 2008
›
  • §66B: Receiving stolen computer resource — 3 years
  • §66C: Identity theft (passwords, Aadhaar) — 3 years + ₹1L
  • §66D: Cheating by impersonation online — 3 years + ₹1L
  • §66E: Privacy violation (private images) — 3 years + ₹2L
  • §66F: Cyber terrorism — Life Imprisonment
§67–67B
Obscene Content & Child Pornography Online
CSAM — strict liability up to 7 years
›
  • §67: Obscene material online — up to 5 years
  • §67A: Sexually explicit material — up to 7 years
  • §67B: Child pornography (CSAM) — up to 7 years, strict liability
§79
Safe Harbour for Intermediaries
Liability protection for platforms & ISPs
›

Exempts intermediaries from third-party content liability if they follow due diligence and act on knowledge of unlawful content. Safe harbour lost if platform conspires or aids the illegal act.

DPDP ACT 2023

Digital Personal Data Protection Act

enacted August 2023

India's first comprehensive data protection law. Governs processing of digital personal data with individual rights and corporate obligations.

// Quick Facts
Max Penalty₹250 Cr
RegulatorDPBI
Child Age<18 yrs
ExtraterritorialYes
§4
Grounds for Processing — Consent
Free, specific, informed, unconditional consent
›

Personal data may only be processed with free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous consent. Consent must be as easy to withdraw as to give.

  • Legitimate uses: law compliance, medical emergencies, employment
  • Data cannot be processed beyond specified purpose
§6–8
Your Rights as a Data Principal
Access, correction, erasure, grievance
›
  • Right to Information: Know what data is processed and by whom
  • Right to Correction: Fix inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Delete data when purpose is served
  • Right to Grievance: Timely complaint resolution
  • Right to Nomination: Nominate someone in case of death/incapacity
§9–16
Obligations of Data Fiduciaries
What companies must do with your data
›
  • Maintain accuracy and completeness of personal data
  • Implement reasonable security safeguards against breaches
  • Children's data: obtain verifiable parental consent; no profiling
  • Mandatory breach notification to Data Protection Board
  • Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint resident DPO
⚠ PenaltiesSecurity failure: ₹250 Cr | Breach notification failure: ₹200 Cr | Children's data: ₹200 Cr
IT RULES 2021

IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules

notified February 2021

Governs social media platforms, digital news portals and OTT platforms. Imposes due diligence, traceability and grievance redressal obligations.

// Quick Facts
Large SMI5M+ users
Complaint ACK24 hours
Resolution15 days
CERT-In Reporting6 hours
Rule 3
Due Diligence for All Intermediaries
Basic obligations for every platform
›
  • Publish rules, privacy policy and user agreement prominently
  • Appoint a Grievance Officer — acknowledge in 24 hours, resolve in 15 days
  • Remove unlawful content within 36 hours of court/government order
  • Retain data for 180 days after account removal
Rule 4
Significant Social Media Intermediaries
Enhanced obligations for 5M+ user platforms
›
  • Chief Compliance Officer — Indian resident, personally liable
  • Nodal Contact Person — 24/7 for law enforcement coordination
  • Monthly public compliance reports with takedown statistics
  • Traceability of first message originator on court order
  • Proactive removal of CSAM and other specified content
CERT-In 2022
CERT-In Mandatory Directions
Incident reporting in 6 hours
›
  • Report cybersecurity incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours
  • Synchronise clocks with Indian Standard Time NTP servers
  • Maintain ICT system logs for 180 days
  • VPN providers: maintain user logs for 5 years
⚠ Non-ComplianceImprisonment up to 1 year and/or fine up to ₹1,00,000 under Section 70B IT Act.
// LANDMARK JUDGEMENTS

Important Case Laws

Landmark judgements that shaped cybersecurity law and digital rights in India.

Supreme Court · Free Speech
Shreya Singhal v. Union of India
AIR 2015 SC 1523 | (2015) 5 SCC 1
Section 66A IT Act challenged for criminalising "grossly offensive" online communication as unconstitutionally vague.
HELDSection 66A struck down — violates Art. 19(1)(a). Landmark free speech victory. Still misused by police post-2015.
Supreme Court · Privacy
Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India
(2017) 10 SCC 1
9-judge Bench examined constitutional status of privacy in the context of Aadhaar biometric data collection.
HELDPrivacy is a Fundamental Right under Article 21. Foundation of DPDP Act 2023. Aadhaar use by private entities struck down.
Supreme Court · Internet Rights
Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India
(2020) 3 SCC 637
Challenged prolonged internet shutdown in J&K post Article 370 revocation as violating fundamental rights.
HELDInternet access is fundamental under Art. 19. Shutdowns must be temporary, necessary & proportionate. Indefinite shutdowns unconstitutional.
Supreme Court · Phone Tapping
PUCL v. Union of India
AIR 1997 SC 568
Legality of telephone tapping under Indian Telegraph Act and extent of state surveillance powers.
HELDPhone tapping is serious invasion of privacy. Procedural safeguards mandatory before interception — paved the way for Sections 69–69B IT Act.
Bombay HC · FCU
Agij Promotion v. UoI (Kunal Kamra)
WP(L)/8932/2023 | Bombay HC
IT Amendment Rules 2023 challenged — government Fact Check Unit to flag "false" content about itself.
HELDFCU provision struck down by Supreme Court — government cannot be sole arbiter of truth about itself. Violates Art. 19(1)(a).
Delhi HC · Intermediary Liability
Super Cassettes v. MySpace Inc.
2011 (47) PTC 49 (Del)
MySpace sued for hosting copyright-infringing user-uploaded content — Section 79 safe harbour examined.
HELDSafe harbour lost once platform has actual knowledge of infringing content. Must act to remove it expeditiously.
Supreme Court · Encryption
WhatsApp LLC v. Union of India
WP(C) No. 44/2019 | SC
WhatsApp challenged IT Rules 2021 traceability requirement conflicting with end-to-end encryption.
HELDCase pending — court recognised tension between traceability and privacy rights. Encryption vs. security debate ongoing.
Delhi HC · Blocking
Foundation for Media Professionals v. UoI
WP(C) 5765/2020 | Delhi HC
Challenged blocking orders under Section 69A against social media accounts during farm protest coverage.
HELDBlocking orders must be proportionate. Originator must get opportunity to respond. Procedural safeguards reinforced.

साइबर सुरक्षा जागरूकता

भारत के साइबर कानूनों और डिजिटल सुरक्षा के बारे में सरल हिंदी में जानकारी — अपने परिवार को share करें।

IT अधिनियम — धारा 66C
पहचान की चोरी (Identity Theft)
कोई आपका पासवर्ड, Aadhaar number या OTP use करे तो यह Identity Theft है। धारा 66C के तहत 3 साल तक की जेल और ₹1 लाख का जुर्माना।
⚠ OTP कभी न दें — बैंक को भी नहीं।
IT अधिनियम — धारा 66E
निजता का उल्लंघन
बिना सहमति किसी की private photos लेना, share करना या morph करना धारा 66E के तहत अपराध। Deepfakes और morphed pictures भी शामिल।
⚠ दंड: 3 साल कैद + ₹2 लाख जुर्माना।
DPDP अधिनियम 2023
आपके डेटा के अधिकार
DPDP Act 2023 के तहत आपको अपना data देखने, सुधारने और delete करने का अधिकार है। कोई भी company आपकी permission के बिना data use नहीं कर सकती।
⚠ उल्लंघन पर ₹250 करोड़ तक जुर्माना।
Digital Arrest — सबसे बड़ा Fraud
Digital Arrest कोई कानून नहीं!
CBI/Police बनकर Video Call पर "Digital Arrest" बताना पूरा fraud है। ऐसा कोई कानून India में है ही नहीं। PM Modi ने भी यही कहा है।
⚠ Call तुरंत काटें। 1930 पर call करें।
IT नियम 2021
Social Media पर आपके अधिकार
IT Rules 2021 के तहत सभी बड़े social media platforms को 24 घंटे में complaint स्वीकार करनी होती है और 15 दिन में हल करना होता है।
⚠ शिकायत: grievance.meity.gov.in
Shreya Singhal Case 2015
Internet पर बोलने की आज़ादी
Supreme Court ने 2015 में Shreya Singhal मामले में कहा — internet पर अभिव्यक्ति की स्वतंत्रता Article 19(1)(a) के तहत fundamental right है।

// DIGITAL SAFETY

Cyber Safety Best Practices

Practical steps every digital citizen should follow to stay safe online.

🔒
Strong Authentication
Use 12+ character passwords with letters, numbers and symbols. Enable 2FA on all critical accounts. Never reuse passwords across platforms.
🎣
Phishing Awareness
Verify sender addresses carefully. Hover links before clicking. Banks and UIDAI never ask for OTPs via call or SMS. Report phishing to CERT-In.
📱
App Permissions
Audit app permissions regularly. Revoke unnecessary camera, microphone, location and contact access. DPDP Act 2023 gives you the right to withdraw consent.
💳
UPI & Financial Safety
Enable transaction alerts. Never share OTPs. QR code scan only for paying, never to receive. If defrauded, call 1930 immediately.
🛡️
Device Security
Keep OS and apps updated. Install reputable antivirus. Encrypt storage. Use VPN on public Wi-Fi. Enable remote wipe for lost devices.
⚖️
Know Your Rights
Under DPDP Act 2023, you can access, correct and erase your data. Challenge companies that collect excessive data. File with DPBI if violated.
👶
Child Online Safety
Enable parental controls. DPDP Act requires parental consent for under-18 data processing. CSAM is a cognisable offence under Section 67B IT Act.
🏢
Corporate Compliance
Implement ISO 27001 under S.43A. Notify CERT-In of breaches within 6 hours. Appoint a Data Protection Officer. DPDP non-compliance = ₹250 Cr fine.
